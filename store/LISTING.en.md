# FocusQuota — Chrome Web Store listing (English)

> Long description for the store backend. Raw markdown, paste as-is.

```markdown
FocusQuota — a gentle daily quota for casual browsing.

You don't need another app that locks you out of websites. You need an honest nudge that tells you the truth: "you've already spent 2 hours scrolling today."

FocusQuota is a lightweight time tracker for casual browsing in Chrome. Set a daily screen-time limit (default: 2 hours) and go about your day. A badge on the toolbar quietly counts down your remaining time. When the quota is used up, you get one friendly reminder for the day — plus a gentle nudge each time you open a new page after that. No blocked pages, no closed tabs, no passwords, no guilt trips. Just awareness.

HOW IT WORKS

- Set your daily quota for casual browsing
- Whitelist the sites that don't count: work tools, AI assistants, local dev servers
- The toolbar badge shows your remaining time at a glance — turning red to show how many minutes over the quota once it's used up
- Reach the limit? A gentle reminder, and a nudge on each new page after that. Nothing is ever blocked.

SOFTER THAN A POMODORO, KINDER THAN A BLOCKER

A Pomodoro timer slices your day into strict 25-minute sprints — great for focused work sessions, exhausting as an all-day regime. A website blocker takes control away from you entirely. FocusQuota is the third option: all-day, soft awareness. It reminds; it never restricts.

WHO THIS IS FOR

- You lose track of time on social media, news sites, and forums
- You want a daily screen-time limit for casual browsing
- Website blockers feel too aggressive; Pomodoro timers feel too rigid for the whole day

WHO THIS IS NOT FOR

- Need a strict Pomodoro timer with focus modes? This isn't it.
- Need to forcibly block distracting sites? A blocker will serve you better.

PRIVACY

- 100% local — everything stays in your browser (chrome.storage.local)
- No accounts, no analytics, no network requests
- Only the minimum permissions needed to do the job
- Open source under GPL-3.0: https://github.com/Achillesy/JavaScript-FocusQuota
```
